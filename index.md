# Mehmet Eren Ahsen

Assistant Professor, Gies College of Business, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning, healthcare, information systems, and operations**. I develop and apply data-driven methods to problems in healthcare and society, with interests in machine learning, causal inference, computational biology, and decision support.

## Featured research

### Individual Versus Institutional Philanthropy: Crowdfunding During Crises

Our recent paper studies how individual and institutional donors respond when a global crisis changes the demand for resources. The study finds a striking contrast between institutional and individual giving during the COVID-19 pandemic and shows why donor heterogeneity matters for crisis-time resource mobilization.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1177/10591478251390971)
