# About Me

Hello! This is Eren. I am an Assistant Professor in Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. In today’s world, data is becoming extremely abundant and data-driven insights are becoming status quo for decisions including those in business and healthcare. My passion is to design and use data analytics tools to guide the decision-making process.

My background in mathematics adds some theoretical flavor to my research. I got a B.S. from the Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department at Middle East Technical University and a second B.S. from the Department of Mathematics at the same university. Studying Mathematics was one of the most important cornerstones in my scientific journey.

I received my M.S. degree from Bilkent University in Control Theory with Prof. Hitay Ozbay before moving to the University of Texas at Dallas for my Ph.D. At UTD I worked with Prof. Mathukumalli Vidyasagar on topics including machine learning, compressed sensing, and computational biology. From there I moved to IBM as a Postdoc and was mentored by Gustavo Stolovitzky, with whom I worked on crowd-sourcing, machine learning, and cancer biology.

Before coming to Gies, I was an assistant professor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai Hospital.
