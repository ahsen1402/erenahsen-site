# About Me

I am Mehmet Eren Ahsen, an Assistant Professor at the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with an interdisciplinary background spanning machine learning, healthcare, information systems, and operations.

My research focuses on developing and applying data-driven and machine-learning methods to important problems in healthcare and society. My work has appeared in outlets including the *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, *PNAS*, *Nature Communications*, *Scientific Reports*, *Information Systems Research*, and other peer-reviewed journals.

I also work on healthcare analytics, artificial intelligence, causal inference, computational biology, and decision support, and I am interested in interdisciplinary research that connects technical methods with real-world organizational and healthcare problems.

For a current publication list, please visit my [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page or [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vFo_xZAAAAAJ&hl=tr&oi=ao).
